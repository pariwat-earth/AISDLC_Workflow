# Extension — Marketing role (optional)

Not part of the development pipeline. Turn it on by listing it in `PROJECT.md`.

**Marketing** analyses the market and competitors · cost · price · sales channels · promotion · trust · retention. It runs in parallel with the pipeline and blocks no stage.

- Output: `Marketing/marketing_plan.md` in the project folder.
- A proposal that changes price or packages changes the requirement: fix `BA/requirement.md` per CLAUDE.md section 4.1 and get the human's approval.
- Reports end with the hand-off block like every other role (CLAUDE.md section 4).
