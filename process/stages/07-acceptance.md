# Stage 7 — Final acceptance

## Who
The human (Decider), and the Client when the work is for someone else.

## How
1. PM gives the human the go/no-go summary — skill `release-readiness`: what was built against the requirement, test and CI results, open accepted risks, anything not done.
2. The human uses the system on the test environment in each role.
3. Feedback → back to the stage it belongs to (requirement, design, or code), with the source document fixed per CLAUDE.md section 4.1.

## Output
A row in `APPROVALS.md`.

## Done when
The human types their approval.

## Human approves
The system as a whole, for release.

## Next
Stage 8 — deploy. PM prepares; the human performs the production steps.
